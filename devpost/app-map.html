RoleFit AI — Technical Map

A reference guide connecting user interactions, deterministic matching formulas, and codebase structure.

1. Application Purpose & Core Journey

RoleFit AI is a proof-of-concept web application built for the Devpost Build With AI: Basics hackathon. It replaces opaque, non-deterministic AI job matching with a pure, explainable 50/35/15 mathematical heuristic, actionable skill gap analysis, and grounded CV adaptation advice.

Key Constraints: Ephemeral in-memory RAM processing (no files or text saved to disk/DB), 5 curated synthetic job listings, and zero live automated scraping or applications.

3. Code Route & Critical Stops

Stop 1: Ephemeral Parsing & Word-Boundary Ontology src/app/api/parse-cv/route.ts

Enforces a strict 5 MB buffer limit before parsing in RAM with pdf-parse and mammoth. Feeds plain text into lib/extractProfile.ts, which uses regex word boundaries (e.g. \bGo\b, \bC\+\+\b) to eliminate false positives without saving documents to disk.

Stop 2: Deterministic 50/35/15 Mathematical Engine src/lib/matchingEngine.ts

Contains pure mathematical functions: calculateSkillsScore() (40 pts required + 10 pts preferred), calculateExperienceScore() (25 pts domain + 10 pts implementation depth), and calculateLocationScore() (10 pts format + 5 pts location). Generates grounded CV adaptation advice linked directly to verified candidate projects.

Stop 3: Explainable Job Cards & Decision Hierarchy src/components/JobCard.tsx

Presents ranked results with the top role automatically expanded. Organizes the accordion drawer around 3 decision questions: What is this role? (unboxed context), Why does it match me? (borderless 50/35/15 meters & match tags), and What should I do next? (CV adaptation tip & Prepare Application action).

Stop 4: Demonstration Application Assistant & Safeguards src/components/ApplicationAssistantModal.tsx

Drafts grounded cover letters and tailored resume wording strictly from candidate project facts. Enforces the non-negotiable DEMO MODE — NOT SUBMITTED badge and submission rejection guard via LocalDemoApplicationAdapter.

4. “To Change This, Start Here”

  • Adjust scoring weights or rounding rules: src/lib/matchingEngine.ts → scoreJob(), calculateSkillsScore()
  • Add or modify synthetic job listings: src/data/demo-jobs.json (5 realistic synthetic roles)
  • Update the pre-loaded 1-click sample candidate: src/data/sample-candidate.ts (Alex Rivera profile)
  • Expand recognized technical skills ontology: src/lib/extractProfile.ts → CANONICAL_SKILLS dictionary
  • Adjust theme styling or color palette: src/app/globals.css & tailwind.config.ts (warm neutral palette)

A Practice to Reuse: Deterministic Logic for Trustworthy AI Products

In AI-assisted career applications, users and reviewers quickly lose trust in opaque, non-deterministic LLM scoring. By formalizing the matching kernel as a pure mathematical formula (50/35/15) with automated test invariants (bounds, ranking, and gap partitioning), RoleFit AI guarantees reproducible results, zero hallucinated skills, and zero risk of leaking private resume text to third-party endpoints.
